Between:
Ravensdale Packing B.V., registered in Bleiswijk, Tuinbouwlaan 12,

[worker name removed], date of birth [removed],
hereinafter "the employee".

Article 1 - Position and start
1.1 The employee enters the employment of the employer on 1 March in the position of
production operator.
1.2 This contract is concluded for a fixed term of twelve months. It is a direct
contract with the employer; the employee is not placed at the disposal of a third
party.

Article 2 - Working hours
2.1 The agreed working time is 36 hours per week, spread over four or five days by
mutual arrangement.
2.2 Overtime is paid at 130% of the hourly wage.

Article 3 - Pay
3.1 The gross hourly wage is EUR 16,80 per hour, in accordance with the collective
agreement that applies to the employer.
3.2 The wage is transferred monthly, before the last working day of the month, to a
bank account in the employee's own name.
3.3 The employee is entitled to 8% holiday allowance on the gross wage.
3.4 No part of the wage is paid out as an expense allowance.

Article 4 - Holiday
4.1 The employee is entitled to 25 holiday days per year on a full-time basis.

Article 5 - Notice
5.1 Either party may end this contract early by giving one (1) month's notice.
5.2 Notice is given in writing, with effect from the end of a calendar month. The same
notice period applies to both parties.

Article 6 - Accommodation
6.1 The employer will, on request, help the employee find accommodation, but is not a
party to any tenancy agreement.
6.2 A tenancy agreement is concluded directly between the employee and the landlord and
is independent of this employment contract. The end of the employment has no
consequences for the accommodation, and the employer charges nothing for housing.

Article 7 - Deductions
7.1 Only the deductions required by law are applied to the wage.
7.2 Any other deduction is made only after a separate written authorisation stating the
amount and the period, which the employee may withdraw.

Article 8 - Work clothing and equipment
8.1 The employer provides work clothing and safety shoes free of charge. No costs are
charged for normal wear, loss or damage.

Article 9 - Other work
9.1 Work for another employer is permitted, provided it does not interfere with the
performance of this position.

Article 10 - Documents
10.1 The employer takes a copy of the employee's identity document for its records and
returns the document itself immediately. The employer does not keep the document.
