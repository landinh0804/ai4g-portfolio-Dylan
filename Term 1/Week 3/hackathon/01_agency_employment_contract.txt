TEMPORARY AGENCY EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT (PHASE A, WITH AGENCY CLAUSE)
English translation provided by the agency. The Dutch text prevails.

Between:
Northfield Staffing Solutions B.V., registered in Zoetermeer, Industrieweg 44,
Chamber of Commerce number 61234567, hereinafter "the agency",

and:
[worker name removed], date of birth [removed],
hereinafter "the agency worker".

Article 1 - Nature of this contract
1.1 The agency worker enters the employment of the agency in order to be placed at
the disposal of a client company.
1.2 This contract is concluded in phase A and includes the agency clause.

Article 2 - Working hours
2.1 No minimum number of working hours per week is guaranteed. The agency worker is
called upon according to the needs of the client company.
2.2 The agency worker is obliged to respond to a call.

Article 3 - Duration and ending
3.1 This contract starts on the first day of the placement.
3.2 The employment contract ends by operation of law on the same day that the
placement ends at the request of the client company, without notice being required.
3.3 The agency worker must give one (1) month's notice in order to end this contract.
3.4 The agency may end the placement by giving two (2) working days' notice.

Article 4 - Pay
4.1 The gross hourly wage is EUR 14,20 per hour worked.
4.2 Travel time and waiting time at the location do not count as hours worked.
4.3 Part of the pay is paid out as an untaxed allowance for living expenses, amounting
to EUR 1,85 per hour worked. This part does not count towards the pension basis.

Article 5 - Placement costs
5.1 For recruitment, selection and placement the agency worker owes a one-off
placement fee of EUR 295,00, to be settled by deduction from the first wage payment.

Article 6 - Advance for travel costs
6.1 The agency provides an advance of EUR 375,00 to cover the journey to the
Netherlands and the application for documents.
6.2 The advance is repaid by a deduction of EUR 95,00 per month from the net wage
until it has been repaid in full.
6.3 If this contract ends within six months, the remaining amount becomes payable
immediately and in full.

Article 7 - Accommodation, transport and insurance
7.1 The agency makes accommodation available in connection with the placement. The
conditions are set out in the accommodation annex.
7.2 Transport to and from the work location is arranged by the agency for a charge of
EUR 75,00 per month. The right to transport lapses when the placement ends.
7.3 The agency worker participates in the collective health insurance arranged through
the agency. The premium of EUR 150,00 per month is deducted from the wage.
Participation ends at the end of the month in which the employment contract ends.

Article 8 - Other work
8.1 During this contract the agency worker may not carry out work for third parties,
paid or unpaid, without the prior written permission of the agency.

Article 9 - Taking up employment with the client company
9.1 If the agency worker enters the employment of the client company, or of a company
affiliated with it, within twelve months after the end of the placement, the agency
worker owes the agency compensation of EUR 2.000,00 for lost income.

Article 10 - Company property
10.1 In the event of loss of or damage to work clothing, safety shoes or an access
pass provided, the agency worker owes an amount of EUR 125,00 per item, to be
deducted from the next wage payment.

Article 11 - Identity documents
11.1 At the start of the placement the agency worker hands their identity document to
the agency. The document is kept in the administration for the duration of this
contract and is returned when the employment ends.

Agreed and signed at ______________ on ____ - ____ - ________
