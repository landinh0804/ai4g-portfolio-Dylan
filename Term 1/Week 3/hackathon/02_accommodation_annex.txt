ACCOMMODATION ANNEX
Annex to the temporary agency employment contract. English version.

Between:
Northfield Housing Services B.V., registered in Zoetermeer, Industrieweg 44,
Chamber of Commerce number 61234568, hereinafter "the provider",

and:
[worker name removed], hereinafter "the occupant".

Article 1 - What is provided
1.1 The provider makes a bed space available in a shared room at an address to be
designated by the provider. The occupant may be moved to another address.
1.2 The accommodation is made available in connection with the placement of the
occupant by Northfield Staffing Solutions B.V. and is not let separately.

Article 2 - Charge
2.1 The charge for the accommodation is EUR 118,00 per week, including gas, water,
electricity and internet.
2.2 The charge is due for every week in which the accommodation is available to the
occupant, whether or not the occupant has worked that week.

Article 3 - Ending
3.1 The right to use the accommodation ends on the day the employment contract with
Northfield Staffing Solutions B.V. ends, for whatever reason.
3.2 The occupant must vacate the accommodation within two (2) calendar days of that
date and hand in the key.

Article 4 - Authorisation for deductions
4.1 The occupant authorises Northfield Staffing Solutions B.V. to deduct from the
wage the charge referred to in article 2, together with any other amounts owed to the
provider or to the agency, whether named in this annex or not.
4.2 This authorisation is given for an indefinite period, cannot be revoked while any
amount remains outstanding, and is not limited to a maximum amount.
4.3 The occupant agrees that these deductions may be made even where this results in
payment of less than the statutory minimum wage.

Article 5 - House rules
5.1 The provider or the agency may enter the accommodation for inspection without
prior notice.
5.2 Visitors staying overnight are not permitted.

Agreed and signed at ______________ on ____ - ____ - ________
